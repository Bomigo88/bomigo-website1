About Bomigo

Built around thoughtful product partnerships.

Bomigo works with wholesale buyers, distributors and brand owners seeking heating mat products and private-label sourcing support.

Our story

Operating in China since 2008.

Bomigo specializes in OEM and ODM manufacturing of infrared and PEMF heating mats, crystal and ceramic heating mats, heating belts and heating pillows. Our factory has operated in China since 2008, supporting product development and wholesale sourcing for international partners.
